Tri-Co Van Rules

  • Tri-co Vehicles are equipped with a wheelchair lift.
  • Passengers are required to show their Bi-Co or Tri-Co ID to ride the bus.
  • Tri-Co vehicles pick up and drop off at Parrish Hall Circle on weekdays until 7 p.m.
  • Tri-Co vehicles pick up and drop off at the Bond Parking lot after 7 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends.
  • Tri-Co shuttle vehicles pick up and drop off at Bryn Mawr's Pembroke Arch and Haverford's Stokes Bay only.
  • Safety rules dictate that no standees on a Swarthmore Van.
  • Passengers may not bring containers of alcoholic beverages (even unopened) onto College buses and vans.
  • The driver has the right to refuse to transport a passenger who is unable or unwilling to control her/his behavior.

Last revised August 6, 2025
