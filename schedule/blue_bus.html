Blue Bus Rules

  • Buses are equipped with a wheelchair lift.
  • Passengers are required to show their Bi-Co or Tri-Co ID to ride the bus.
  • The Bi-Co Blue Bus and vans pick up and drop off at Bryn Mawr's Pembroke Arch Bus Stop and Stokes Bay at Haverford College.
  • Safety rules dictate that no more than 42 passengers and 15 standees may ride the bus per trip.
  • Passengers may not bring containers of alcoholic beverages (even unopened) onto College buses and vans.
  • The driver has the right to refuse to transport a passenger who is unable or unwilling to control his/her behavior.
  • When only one Blue Bus is running, the driver leaves as soon as the bus is full.
